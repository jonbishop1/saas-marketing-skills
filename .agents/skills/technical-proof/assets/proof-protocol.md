# Technical proof protocol

## Claim and scope

- Proposed claim and type: functional / performance / cost / customer outcome:
- Reader decision the evidence should support:
- Task, workload, baseline, and population:
- Narrowest claim the method could support:

## Reproducibility

- Versions, configuration, hardware or service tier:
- Dataset source, license, permission, shape, scale, and skew:
- Correctness and semantic equivalence checks:
- Warm/cold state, concurrency, repetitions, and measurement method:
- Failure inclusion and exclusion policy chosen before results:
- Resource limits, cost accounting, and cleanup:
- Authorized execution scope:

## Results, only after execution

| Run or cohort | Correctness | Measurement | Cost if relevant | Failure/deviation | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Report variability and meaningful failures. If the protocol has not run, leave result cells empty and call this a plan.

## Publication gate

- Claim supported by the actual results:
- Conditions and limitations adjacent to the claim:
- Unfavorable results or tradeoffs included:
- Reproduction path and source licenses:
- Customer permission and quote approval where relevant:
- Private details removed without implying invented evidence:
