# Creative test card

Audience hypothesis / demonstrated task / creative concept / substantiated claim / offer / next product action / qualification event / cap / decision rule.

## Fictional concepts

**Developer tool:** a short, verified screen recording of sanitizing and replaying one request. Show the supported environment and link to the example. A view is engagement, not activation.

**Data product:** an illustrative diagram explaining a late-arrival reconciliation failure, followed by an evaluation worksheet. Use synthetic records and label them. A download is not evidence of deployment fit.

## Review

Compare concepts on the same defined outcome and acknowledge uncertainty when traffic is small. A creative that attracts fewer visitors but more qualified evaluations may be useful; do not declare this effect without the cohort data.
