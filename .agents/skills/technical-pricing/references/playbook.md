# Meter comparison

Meter / customer value relationship / customer control / cost driver relationship / predictability / implementation needs / likely failure mode.

## Illustrative workload arithmetic

If a hypothetical plan includes 100 units and charges $2 per additional unit, a 140-unit workload costs base price + $80, before any stated taxes or separate charges. Define the unit and rounding. These are fictional inputs for explaining the calculation, not recommended prices.

## Technical gotchas

Clarify retries, failed jobs, cached responses, compressed versus raw bytes, partial hours, background maintenance, and metering delay where relevant. Do not assert behavior the product has not implemented.

## Evaluation boundary

A free sample can lower uncertainty, but a data-intensive real evaluation may need an explicit cap, customer approval, and a cost estimate. Evaluate the tradeoff rather than promising unlimited use.
